WalkIt - Current Features

WalkIt is an Android prototype for mapping photo reports and planning routes in the Philippines.

• Opens to an interactive street map focused on the Philippines, with roads and map labels.
• Includes a current-location control that can show the user's position after location permission is granted.
• Provides an in-app camera with Single and Batch capture, plus gallery photo selection.
• After capture, lets the user pan and zoom beneath a fixed center pin to choose where the report belongs.
• Saves a report with photos, description, time, and selected map coordinates.
• Displays reports as circular photo markers. Tapping a marker opens its photos and a discussion panel.
• Discussion comments can be posted anonymously and may include a photo attachment.
• Has a top-right Point A to Point B route planner that accepts map coordinates and requests a route from BRouter. Route calculation requires internet access.
• Includes Map, Forums, and Profile tabs. Forums and Profile are currently empty placeholders.

Storage and sharing

Reports, photos, and comments are stored locally on the device. They are not synchronized with other users or devices because a shared backend and cloud media storage have not been connected.

Build artifact

The repository includes a debug APK at artifacts/WalkIt-debug.apk. This is a development build of the prototype.
